# Core contract

## Evidence gate

An action-ready candidate requires all of the following:

1. At least three independent signals or two different work moments;
2. Diversity across at least two people, roles or work objects;
3. A stated link to a business goal, delivery, quality, cost, risk or user outcome;
4. An active search for counter-evidence such as “already resolved”, “one-off”, “misunderstanding”, or “resource available”;
5. A minimal evidence trail the user can verify.

Otherwise mark it as an observation or hypothesis needing review.

## Required result shape

For every action-ready candidate, provide:

1. Neutral observation;
2. Candidate capability or mechanism gap;
3. Evidence distribution and counter-evidence;
4. Why it may be systemic rather than local;
5. Desired business outcome;
6. Responsible capability units and interfaces;
7. Action portfolio: now, next cycle, conditional long term;
8. Preconditions, risks, success signals and human confirmation questions.
